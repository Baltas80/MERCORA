# Abuse and moderation

MERCORA is a legal second-hand marketplace. The dark visual style does not change the permitted-use policy.

Controls include account suspension, listing moderation, user reports, dispute controls, rate limits, transaction idempotency, seller risk gates and audit records.

Prohibited or restricted goods must be represented by a versioned policy. High-risk categories should enter manual review rather than relying exclusively on keyword matching.

Abuse signals to implement progressively:
- credential stuffing and automated registration;
- spam listings and duplicate content;
- self-trading or coordinated purchases;
- review manipulation;
- repeated frivolous disputes;
- promotion-code abuse;
- payout cycling;
- malicious uploads.

Enforcement actions must be attributable, reversible where appropriate, and auditable. Do not expose hidden risk scores or private moderation information to ordinary users.
