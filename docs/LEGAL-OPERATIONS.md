# Legal and operational gate

This repository implements technical controls but does not itself establish legal compliance.

Before public operation, the operator must obtain jurisdiction-specific advice covering:
- marketplace and consumer obligations;
- Digital Services Act obligations where applicable;
- privacy/GDPR and retention;
- crypto-asset custody/payment classification and authorization where applicable;
- tax/accounting obligations;
- professional-vs-private seller treatment;
- prohibited/restricted goods and intellectual-property rules.

Technical policy tables are versioned so operational changes can be audited. Legal policy changes must be approved outside the code-only workflow and documented with the applicable jurisdiction and effective date.
